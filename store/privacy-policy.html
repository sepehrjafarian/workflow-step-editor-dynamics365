Privacy policy – Workflow Step Editor for Dynamics 365 & CRM

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary

The extension collects no data. Everything it reads stays between your browser and your own Dynamics server.

What the extension does

The extension runs only on the classic process designer page of Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Dynamics CRM (…/sfa/workflow/edit.aspx). When you open its panel, it reads the definition of the process you are editing from your Dynamics server's Web API, using the sign-in you already have in that browser tab. When you click Apply, Cancel or Reset, it writes the process definition back to the same server.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • It does not read or store passwords or other credentials.
  • It does not run on any page other than the process designer.
  • It does not load code from the internet; all of its code ships with the extension.

Storage

The extension stores one temporary value in the browser tab's session storage: a marker that reopens its panel after the page reloads. It is removed as soon as it is used and never leaves your browser.
